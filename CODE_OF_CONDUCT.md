# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in all project spaces — issues,
pull requests, and discussions.

- Critique the design or the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent; keep discussion on technical merit.
- Content should stay professional and on-topic.

Maintainers may moderate contributions that do not follow these rules.
For severe cases, contact a maintainer through the IOWAP org.

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
